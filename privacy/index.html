Day, On Earth: privacy

Effective 11 October 2026

The Day, On Earth app collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. The website has an optional email list, described below.

Your location

To tell you about the weather where you are, the app asks to use your location while you use it (and, for widgets, when they refresh).

  • Your position is rounded to about 2 km on your device before anything else happens. Only the rounded position is ever kept, on your device, and only to know whether you have moved.
  • The rounded position is sent to Apple's WeatherKit to get the forecast. Apple provides the weather data and handles that request under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive it.
  • If you choose a city instead, that city is kept on your device in the same way.

You can turn location off at any time in Settings; the app then asks for a city.

What stays on your device

Your forecast, the sentences you have been shown (so they don't repeat too often), your settings and whether you have bought the unlock are stored only on your device, in the app and its widgets. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase is present on your device.

Sharing

A share card is drawn on your device. It goes only where you send it, and it leaves out your location name unless you switch it on.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

The website's email list

This applies to the website only, and only if you choose to leave your email address there. The app itself still collects nothing.

  • The address you type is stored in a private Google Form and spreadsheet that only the developer can open. Google processes it for us under Google's privacy policy.
  • We use it to send news about Day, On Earth: the launch, and now and then what's new. Nothing else, and we don't sell or share it.
  • To be removed, or to ask what we hold, write to hello@dayonearth.app and the address is deleted.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Anything that collected data would also change the App Store label first.
