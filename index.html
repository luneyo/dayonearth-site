Good to know

Is it free?

The app is free and complete. One purchase, once, puts the full sentence in every widget, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. Without it, widgets show the day's numbers.

Where does the weather come from?

From Apple Weather. Severe-weather warnings come from your country's official weather service, named on the warning.

Does it collect anything about me?

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is rounded to about 2 km and used only to get your forecast. Read the privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, on iOS 18 or later.
